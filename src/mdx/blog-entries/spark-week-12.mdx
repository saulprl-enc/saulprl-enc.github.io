## Certification course
<br />

During this week, I continued with my certification course, where I got to learn a lot regarding AWS Aurora, RDS, Route 53, and S3. For RDS, I mostly saw topics I've already seen in the past, but Aurora was definitely a new one. I didn't get to test it out in practice because of its cost, but learned that it is pretty similar in general. Route 53, though, it was a whole new topic (even though I've worked with DNS before). The main takeaway for me about Route 53 was all of the different routing policies it allows you to set up: sending users to different domains or servers depending on their latency, location, IP, path, parameters, and many more criteria. Finally, I revisited some S3 concepts and got to host a static webpage from it.
