# Spark Week 6
<br />

Week 6 started off pretty rough, but it got so much better pretty quickly. As an overview, we had a couple of meetings, the Breakable Toy's due date, and the demo presentation at the end of the week. Let's go over the meetings first.
<br />

## Meetings
<br />

### Mental Health and Neurodivergencies
<br />

On Monday, we had a meeting regarding *Mental Health and Neurodivergencies* where we went over the importance and impact of mental health not only in our personal lives, but also in our day-to-day work-related activities. I think this is a really relevant topic to talk about when most of our work is remote and we barely get any chances to actually interact with our teammates (it's not the same at all to interact through a screen than to see them in person). For this reason, it can be pretty difficult to know if somebody's going through some struggles unless they openly communicate it to someone else, which can lead to self-isolation, decrease in productivity, and even serious mental health issues like depression.

When it comes to neurodivergencies, I think it's also an important topic due to similar reasons. Being aware of the topic can be really helpful so that people with a neurodivergency don't feel isolated and dismissed. In my own experience, I've gone through the struggle of self-isolating due to feeling like I don't fit in the group.
<br />

### Spark Communication Sessions
<br />

On Friday, we had our first *Spark Communication Sessions*, where Kathia went over the purpose of these sessions and we were given the opportunity to practice speaking in English by talking about ourselves and our experience with public speaking. I enjoyed this meeting because I've always felt more comfortable expressing myself in English (having always had the theory that I have a slightly more expressive personality when not communicating in Spanish), and also because public speaking is definitely something I'd love to get better at (event if I think I can do it fairly decently at the moment).
<br />

## Breakable Toy
<br />

Like I mentioned, Breakable Toy's due date was this Wednesday and I was still working on some details at the start of the week. After getting those finishing touches done, I started focusing on testing, and that's when I decided to flat-out ask my mentor for help. We had a call where we reviewed some of the testing I had already done and he gave me a lot of really helpful insight regarding testing applications in general, and also some specific tips for both front-end and back-end testing. By the end of the call, I felt more confident to write tests since I now had a couple of examples, and started writing tests for the back-end. Front-end testing took me a little more, but eventually felt like I got the hang of it.

Ultimately, I made some more small changes to the app and delivered it. I absolutely feel so much more confidence when it comes to testing.
<br />

## Demo presentation
<br />

I asked my mentor for any recommendations he had for my demo presentation and then started working on it. I followed the layout given to us and decided to keep the presentation as simple as possible, allowing myself to explain the details during the meeting. Overall, I felt confident about my presentation because I'm used to presenting in front of tens and even hundreds of people, and only got a bit nervous a few minutes before starting, but it quickly faded. During the presentation, I felt completely normal, but I did notice that I might have overexplained some sections of the app (or just took too long while giving context in general).
<br />

All in all, it was a pretty good week.
